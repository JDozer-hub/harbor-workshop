Most first-time tilers make the same trip twice: once for the tiles, once for everything they forgot. This is the whole list, in the order you need it, sized for a 5 m² floor with walls tiled to 1.2 m.

The costly mistake is not the tile design — it is cheap adhesive and the wrong grout in a wet area. That is the one place to spend the extra.

Which tier to buy

Cheapest that works

Budget adhesive and a basic spacer pack. Fine on a dry floor area — accept that you may lose a bag to waste. Walmart →

Buy the good one

Powdered adhesive and mould-resistant grout. This is where a tiling job is won or lost; the tile pattern is the cheap part. Home Depot →

Tools you probably already own

Drill with a mixing paddle, Spirit level (600 mm is enough), Tape measure, Bucket, Grout sponge, Tile cutter or wet saw, Utility knife, Knee pads, Gloves. Do not buy these new for one job.

What to skip, and why

Ready-mixed adhesive for a shower

It cures by drying, not by chemical set. In a permanently damp wet area it can stay soft and let tiles move. Powdered adhesive is mixed with water and sets hard — it costs about the same.

A full wet-saw for one small bathroom

Rent it or borrow it. Buying a saw you will use once can cost more than the tiles. A manual cutter plus a diamond file handles straight cuts and the few awkward ones.

Mosaic border strips bought at the same time as the tile

They lock you into the layout before you have measured your actual cuts. Lay the field tile first, then decide whether an accent is worth the extra cutting.

How the quantities were worked out

Floor: the stated room is 5 m². Tile is bought in boxes covering about 0.99 m², so 5 ÷ 0.99 = 5.05 boxes, rounded up to 6 to absorb cuts and breakages.

Wall: two long walls at 2.5 m and two short at 2 m, tiled to 1.2 m high, is (2.5+2.5+2+2) × 1.2 = 10.8 m², minus roughly 1.8 m² for the door and window, giving about 9 m². At about 1 m² per box that is 9 boxes before waste.

Adhesive: a 20 kg bag at a 3 mm bed covers roughly 5 m², so a 9 m² wall plus a 5 m² floor needs about 3 bags.

Quantities assume the room dimensions stated in the opening paragraph. Recalculate from your own measurements before ordering.
