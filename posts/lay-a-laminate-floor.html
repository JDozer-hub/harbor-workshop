Laminate is a floating floor: it is not fixed down, and it expands. Almost every laminate failure is either an uneven subfloor or a missing expansion gap.

This list is sized for a 20 m² room and assumes you are laying over an existing hard floor rather than removing it.

Which tier to buy

Cheapest that works

Thin foam underlay and a basic laminate. Honest for a low-traffic spare room; it will sound hollow and feel thin underfoot. Walmart →

Buy the good one

A thicker acoustic underlay and a laminate with a decent wear layer. The underlay is what removes the hollow click and takes up small subfloor imperfections, so it does more for how the floor feels than the board does. Home Depot →

Tools you probably already own

Tapping block, Pull bar, Spacers, Utility knife, Hand saw or jigsaw, Tape measure, Knee pads, Pencil. Do not buy these new for one job.

What to skip, and why

Leaving no expansion gap at the walls

The floor grows with humidity. Without a gap it lifts at the middle or buckles at a doorway. The gap is not cosmetic — the skirting hides it.

Laying over an uneven concrete floor without checking

Laminate flexes over humps and the joints fail. Check flatness with a long straight edge; the pack states the tolerance. Levelling compound is much cheaper than replacing a floor.

Laying laminate in a bathroom or a wet room

Even water-resistant laminate has a core that swells at the edges. For a bathroom, use a product actually rated for wet areas, or a different floor entirely.

How the quantities were worked out

Packs: a 20 m² room plus 10% waste is 22 m². At roughly 2.2 m² per pack that is 10 packs. Buy at least one spare pack and keep it — a discontinued board is almost impossible to match later.

Perimeter gap: allow about 10 mm at every wall for a 20 m² room, which is what the spacers are for. That is why skirting or scotia is on the list rather than optional.

Acclimatise the packs flat in the room for about 48 hours before laying. Boards laid straight from a cold store can shrink after installation and open up at the joints.
